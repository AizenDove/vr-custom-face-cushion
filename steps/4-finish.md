# 4. Finish and test the fit

<!-- PHOTO: before and after sanding -->

## Clean up

1. Remove the supports carefully. Flush cutters help with TPU.
2. Sand every edge and surface that touches your face until it's smooth.
3. Wash off the dust.

## Assemble

If you printed a PLA part and a TPU part, join them now.

<!-- TODO: how the parts join -->

## Test

1. Attach the gasket to the headset and put it on.
2. Check for:
   - **Pressure points**: spots that press or hurt after a few minutes.
   - **Light leaks**: gaps where light gets in, usually around the nose.
   - **Lens distance**: your eyes shouldn't be closer to the lenses than with the stock gasket.
3. Wear it for a longer session before you call it done.

## Adjust

Mark problem spots, go back to [Blender](2-blender.md), push the face in more or less there, and print again. It usually takes a couple of rounds.
